## Findings

**LOCATION**: Opening sentence — "I write to you by the light of a tallow candle that smokes worse than the stationmaster's pipe, having just returned from the Kuzmin household, where I was summoned at midnight to attend a case of what the old grandmother insisted was 'possession by a forest spirit' and what I, after an hour's examination, determined to be a rather ordinary case of indigestion brought on by too much salted mushroom."
**WHAT**: A single ~90-word periodic sentence, three levels of subordination deep, architected toward a punchline reveal (folk diagnosis → clinical deflation). Absent from all ten corpus letter-openers.
**CORPUS EVIDENCE**: Every sample opens flat and direct — "I got your letter when I was fearfully bored" (01), "Did you have rain when Ivan was coming back from the monastery?" (03), "The Amur is a very fine river" (04), "When I got to Rome I went to the post-office and did not find a single letter" (05). None builds a scene through nested subordinate clauses before landing a reveal.
**CONFIDENCE**: high

**LOCATION**: Whole draft — zero instances of "!", "?", or "...".
**WHAT**: The draft has no exclamations, no direct questions to the recipient, and no trailing ellipses anywhere in ~500 words.
**CORPUS EVIDENCE**: All ten samples use these constantly and for the same jobs the draft avoids — trailing off ("Rejoice, O mother!... give me three roubles and an ounce of tea" 03; "Aie, aie!" 09), comic/emphatic exclamation ("Oh, the caviare! I eat it and eat and never have enough." 03; "What brilliance!" 05), and direct address-as-question to the recipient ("Is the mongoose alive?" 05; "Isn't it revolting? Isn't it disgusting?" 03). This is not occasional — it recurs in every single sample.
**CONFIDENCE**: high

**LOCATION**: "I live vicariously through your letters, Pavel Sergeyevich, the way a starving man reads a menu."
**WHAT**: A self-contained, portable aphorism/simile not anchored to a specific observed thing.
**CORPUS EVIDENCE**: The corpus's similes are tied to something the writer has just seen — steam-tugs "like some fine young intellectual trying to run away while a plebeian wife... hold on to his coat-tails" (03), cabmen "like Dobrolyubov" (03), gulls "looking like the younger Drishka" (03). The nearest corpus counterpart to a self-directed simile, "I am in the condition of a transplanted tree" (09), is still built out of the letter's own material, not a free-floating proverb.
**CONFIDENCE**: low
