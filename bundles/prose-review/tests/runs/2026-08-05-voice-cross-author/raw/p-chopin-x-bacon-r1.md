The deviation here is not span-level. The whole draft sits outside the corpus, so the findings
below cite representative spans rather than isolated ones.

**LOCATION**: draft.txt lines 41–43 — "In the discharge of thy place, set before thee the best
examples; for imitation is a globe of precepts, and after a time set before thee thine own
example; and examine thyself strictly whether thou didst not best at first."

**WHAT**: The draft addresses a reader directly in second-person imperative, instructing him
how to conduct himself; the corpus never addresses a reader at all.

**CORPUS EVIDENCE**: All ten samples are third-person narration with an effaced narrator.
Where the corpus reaches for generalisation it stays in third person and in the exclamatory,
not the imperative: "But the beginning of things, of a world especially, is necessarily vague,
tangled, chaotic, and exceedingly disturbing. How few of us ever emerge from such beginning!"
(sample-02, lines 20–22). That is the corpus's single most essayistic passage and it still
neither says "thou" nor tells the reader to do anything. Second-person instruction appears in
the corpus only inside quoted dialogue between characters ("Pontellier... let your wife alone
for a while", sample-06, lines 92–93), never in the narrating voice. The archaic pronouns
thou/thee/thy/thyself are absent from all ten samples.

**CONFIDENCE**: high

**LOCATION**: draft.txt lines 14–23 — "Certainly, great persons had need to borrow other men's
opinions to think themselves happy; for if they judge by their own feeling, they cannot find
it; but if they think with themselves what other men think of them, and that other men would
fain be as they are, then they are happy as it were by report, when, perhaps, they find the
contrary within; for they are the first that find their own griefs, though they be the last
that find their own faults."

**WHAT**: The periodic sentence built by chained semicolons and stacked "for / but / though"
clauses, running past a hundred words and resolving in an antithetical maxim, is a shape absent
from every sample.

**CORPUS EVIDENCE**: The corpus's long sentences branch once from a narrative spine and stop:
"Edna Pontellier could not have told why, wishing to go to the beach with Robert, she should in
the first place have declined, and in the second place have followed in obedience to one of the
two contradictory impulses which impelled her" (sample-02, lines 1–4, ~45 words, one clause
structure). Its longest catalogue sentence is sensory, not argumentative: sample-03, lines
21–23. Nowhere in ten samples does a sentence advance a proposition through successive
adversative clauses. The related maxim shape — balanced antithesis as a closing verdict, e.g.
"severity breedeth fear, but roughness breedeth hate" (line 75–76), "by pains men come to
greater pains" (line 6), "All rising to great place is by a winding stair" (line 92) — also
appears in none of the ten.

**CONFIDENCE**: high

**LOCATION**: draft.txt lines 9–10, 25–27, 37–39, 83–87 — e.g. "Omnium consensu capax imperii,
nisi imperasset," saith Tacitus of Galba"

**WHAT**: Untranslated Latin quoted from named authorities as evidence for a claim, four times,
with the citation formula "saith X" / "as Solomon saith" (line 79).

**CORPUS EVIDENCE**: The corpus does use a second language, but the usage is opposite in kind:
French, short, and always either a character's speech ("_Allez vous-en! Allez vous-en!
Sapristi!_", sample-01, line 4; "_Parbleu!_", sample-06, line 60) or the name of a thing in the
characters' world (_soirée musicale_, _porte cochère_, sample-05; _Chênière Caminada_,
sample-01, line 47). It is local colour, never authority. No sample cites any named source, and
the one biblical touch is unattributed and absorbed into a character's own thought — "Better a
dinner of herbs" (sample-05, line 141) — the reverse of the draft's practice of naming the
authority and leaving the tag in place.

**CONFIDENCE**: high

**LOCATION**: draft.txt lines 61–79 — "The vices of authority are chiefly four: delays,
corruption, roughness, and facility... As for facility, it is worse than bribery"

**WHAT**: Vocabulary of juridical and administrative abstraction — *privateness* (line 12),
*facility* and *importunity* (lines 77–78), *peremptory* (line 53), *jurisdiction* (line 55),
*de facto* (line 56), *sufficiency* (line 87) — used in technical senses at a level of
abstraction the corpus's vocabulary never occupies.

**CORPUS EVIDENCE**: The corpus's abstract nouns are affective and concrete-adjacent rather
than institutional: "the shadowy anguish" (sample-02, line 10), "a certain ungovernable dread"
(sample-03, line 37), "an appalling and hopeless ennui", "life's delirium" (sample-05, lines
157–162), "the first-felt throbbings of desire" (sample-03, line 203). Where the corpus does
touch business language it declines to specify, and says so: "securities, exchanges, stocks,
bonds, or something of the sort, Madame Ratignolle did not remember what" (sample-04, lines
23–24). Naming a taxonomy of four vices and then working through each in order has no
counterpart in any sample. This finding is a consequence of the register break above rather
than independent of it.

**CONFIDENCE**: high

**LOCATION**: whole document, draft.txt lines 1–101

**WHAT**: One tone is held from first line to last — measured, magisterial, evenly confident —
where the corpus visibly changes with what it is doing.

**CORPUS EVIDENCE**: The corpus shifts audibly and often. Flat domestic reportage: "It was not
more than nine o'clock. No one had yet gone to bed but the children" (sample-04, lines 14–15).
Clipped comic dialogue: "'Thought of what?' / 'Of anything. What difference does it make?'"
(sample-03, lines 108–110). Incantatory lyric: "The voice of the sea speaks to the soul"
(sample-02, line 28). Bantering pastiche within a character's speech (sample-03, lines
130–139). These sit inside single samples, not merely across them — sample-03 moves through all
of them in nine pages. The draft never modulates: its account of a spirit of ambition (lines
88–92) is pitched exactly as its instruction about keeping appointments (lines 62–64).

**CONFIDENCE**: high
