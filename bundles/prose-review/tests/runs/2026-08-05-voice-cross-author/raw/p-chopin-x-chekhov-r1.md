**FINDING 1**

- **LOCATION**: draft.txt lines 5–16, e.g. "From early morning I go from hut to hut, and knock
  my head in the low doorways which I can't get used to, and as ill-luck will have it my head
  aches hellishly; I have migraine and influenza."
- **WHAT**: The draft is narrated in the first person by a speaker reporting his own days and
  ailments; the corpus narrates in the third person throughout, with the first person confined
  to quoted speech.
- **CORPUS EVIDENCE**: Across all ten samples there is no first-person narration. Interior
  experience is reported from outside: "She let her mind wander back over her stay at Grand
  Isle" (sample-04, line 46–47); "Edna Pontellier could not have told why … she should in the
  first place have declined" (sample-02, lines 1–3). Where "I" appears it is inside quotation
  marks and belongs to a character — "I thought I should have perished out there alone"
  (sample-03, lines 79–80). This holds without exception in the corpus and is inverted for the
  whole of the draft.
- **CONFIDENCE**: high
