**FINDING 1 — register break (category 1)**

- **LOCATION**: draft.txt lines 1–23, opening paragraph: "Men in great place are thrice
  servants—servants of the sovereign or state, servants of fame, and servants of business; so as
  they have no freedom, neither in their persons, nor in their actions, nor in their times."
- **WHAT**: The draft is impersonal, sententious moral instruction with no narrator, no scene, no
  characters and no dialogue anywhere in its 102 lines, pitched at a far greater distance from the
  reader than any sample in the corpus.
- **CORPUS EVIDENCE**: In all ten samples the author writes as a visible narrator standing next to
  the reader, and turns to address him directly: "I invoke your consideration of the scene"
  (sample-01, l.10); "While she swiftly makes ready, let us discreetly face the other way and
  gossip" (sample-02, ll.117–118); "Perhaps you have seen a pier-glass in an $8 flat" (sample-08,
  ll.42–43); "Will it tire you to be told again that Aileen was beautiful?" (sample-06, l.44).
  Where the corpus does moralise it does so in a brief aside and then breaks the frame with a joke
  against itself — "This story really doesn't get anywhere at all" (sample-02, l.238); "But this
  irrelevant stuff is taking up space that the story should occupy" (sample-02, ll.36–37). Nothing
  of that self-interrupting, reader-facing posture appears anywhere in the draft.
- **CONFIDENCE**: high

**FINDING 2 — construction absent from the corpus (category 2)**

**FINDING 4 — vocabulary outside the corpus range (category 3)**

- **LOCATION**: line 11, "impatient of privateness"; line 47, "Reform, therefore, without bravery

- **CORPUS EVIDENCE**: The corpus reaches for abstraction only to cash it immediately into a

  on an object; its one extended figure, "All rising to great place is by a winding stair" (l.92),
  is illustrative rather than comic and is not followed by a deflation.
- **CONFIDENCE**: high
