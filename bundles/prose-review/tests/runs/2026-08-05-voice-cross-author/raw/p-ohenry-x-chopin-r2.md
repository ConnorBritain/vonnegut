# Voice review — case-05

No voice card supplied; corpus of ten samples read in full before the draft. No
deterministic rhythm scan supplied, so category 4 is not assessed below rather than
guessed at.

---

**LOCATION**: draft.txt lines 1–6 — "They formed a congenial group sitting there that
summer afternoon—Madame Ratignolle sewing away, often stopping to relate a story or
incident with much expressive gesture of her perfect hands"

**WHAT**: The narrator sets a scene without ever turning to the reader, and does so
nowhere in the draft; the corpus narrator addresses the reader directly and does it
while setting scenes in particular.

**CORPUS EVIDENCE**: Present in all ten samples, usually within the first paragraphs.
sample-01 line 10: "I invoke your consideration of the scene—the marble-topped tables";
sample-08 lines 13–14: "While the mistress of the home is gradually subsiding from the
first stage to the second, take a look at the home"; sample-08 line 42: "Perhaps you
have seen a pier-glass in an $8 flat"; sample-06 lines 34–35: "Why do you suggest
Matilda? Please listen this time—Tildy—Tildy"; sample-06 line 44: "Will it tire you to
be told again that Aileen was beautiful?"; sample-02 lines 47–49: "Don't care? Very
well; probably you are interested in larger amounts"; sample-05 line 23: "Say, gentle
reader, did you ever have a 200-pound woman…". The draft has no second-person address
and no first-person narrator at all.

**CONFIDENCE**: high

**LOCATION**: draft.txt lines 44–48 — "Meanwhile Robert, addressing Mrs Pontellier,
continued to tell of his one time hopeless passion for Madame Ratignolle; of sleepless
nights, of consuming flames till the very sea sizzled when he took his daily plunge."

**WHAT**: The narrator moves through the material without ever stepping out of it to
comment on the telling; the corpus narrator interrupts his own story to complain about
it, abandon a calculation, or announce that a character is not worth more paragraphs.

**CORPUS EVIDENCE**: Absent from all ten samples in the draft; present in at least six.
sample-02 lines 36–37: "But this irrelevant stuff is taking up space that the story
should occupy"; sample-02 line 132: "I give it up"; sample-02 lines 150–151: "He is a
type; I can dwell upon him no longer; my pen is not the kind intended for him; I am no
carpenter"; sample-02 line 238: "This story really doesn't get anywhere at all";
sample-04 line 237: "The story should end here"; sample-01 line 67: "It is worth a
paragraph to say that…"; sample-08 lines 87–88: "Oh, and the next two hours tripped by
on rosy wings. Forget the hashed metaphor"; sample-07 lines 109–111: "And though our
tropes of fairyland be mixed with those of entomology…".

**CONFIDENCE**: high

**LOCATION**: draft.txt lines 25–29 — "'Robert, come; go; stand up; sit down; do this;
do that; see if the baby sleeps; my thimble, please, that I left God knows where.'" —
and every other line of quoted speech in the draft (lines 31–37, 50, 78, 126–134).

**WHAT**: Every speaker in the draft talks in the same educated register as the
narration; the corpus routinely puts vernacular, dialect or slang speech in a character's
mouth and derives its effect from the gap between that speech and the narrator's.

**CORPUS EVIDENCE**: Vernacular dialogue appears in all ten samples. sample-03 lines
43–47: "dat gent on de oder bench sent yer a song and dance by me"; sample-06 lines
113–117: "Sashayed up, so he did, and made a break… Gee! but I slapped him a good one";
sample-07 lines 62–63: "Oh, gee!… Swell fellow? well, I guess! Style? Look at 'um";
sample-09 lines 176–178: "Pulled it off… we was under the wire at the Reverend's at
9.30¼"; sample-10 line 22: "'Tis here… I will be diverted"; sample-01 lines 195–197:
"got hot on account of things said about the bum sidewalks"; sample-02 lines 58–62:
"Well, ain't you the lucky one? Piggy's an awful swell"; sample-04 line 262: "I can lick
the man that invented poverty"; sample-05 lines 107–112 (the black-and-tan's speech);
sample-08 lines 79–80: "Take yer hat off and let's have a sight at the looks of it".
The draft contains no instance.

**CONFIDENCE**: high

**LOCATION**: draft.txt lines 66–68 — "seated there like some sensuous Madonna, with the
gleam of the fading day enriching her splendid color"; and lines 118–119 — "with the
grace and majesty which queens are sometimes supposed to possess"; and lines 130–131 —
"whose sonorous murmur reached her like a loving but imperative entreaty".

**WHAT**: The draft's similes reach for beauty and are left standing; the corpus builds
its similes out of deliberately incongruous commercial or domestic material, and often
undercuts them in the same breath.

**CORPUS EVIDENCE**: sample-05 line 29: "looking like a cross between an Angora cat and
a box of lemons"; sample-04 lines 189–190: "as a twenty-six inch maiden fills her
twenty-two inch girdle"; sample-01 lines 16–17: "the Würzburger in the tall glass cones
that bend to your lips as a ripe cherry sways on its branch to the beak of a robber
jay"; sample-06 lines 57–58: "every smile that she sent forth lodged, like pellets from
a scatter-gun, in as many hearts"; sample-09 lines 30–31: "she was the compound extract
of all that was chemically pure and officinal"; sample-06 lines 152–155 (the "one-day
laundry work" conceit). The one elevated comparison the corpus does make earnestly —
sample-08's "rippling and shining like a cascade of brown waters" — sits beside "Forget
the hashed metaphor" eighteen lines later.

**CONFIDENCE**: high

**LOCATION**: draft.txt, whole document — from line 1 to line 139, one sustained tone.

**WHAT**: The draft holds a single elegiac, psychologically observant register
throughout; the corpus visibly shifts register inside a single piece, often between
adjacent paragraphs.

**CORPUS EVIDENCE**: sample-02 opens on a comic theological dream-frame ("God is radium,
or ether or some scientific compound", lines 2–4), breaks into a ledger of Dulcie's
weekly expenses ("The total amounts to $4.76", line 130), rises to the pathos of General
Kitchener's photograph (lines 181–207), and closes on a flat joke about the orphan
asylum. sample-06 moves from mock-heroic ("Though a man should extract a sanguinary
stream from the pallid turnip", lines 13–15) to Tildy sobbing among the butter chips
(lines 218–226). sample-07 sets Dempsey's board of referees as gladiators (lines
154–157) and then closes on "Say—will a duck swim?". The draft never changes gear;
lines 94–101, on the children and the bonbon box, are pitched exactly as lines 103–106
on the breeze.

**CONFIDENCE**: high

**LOCATION**: draft.txt lines 52–58 — "She never knew precisely what to make of it; at
that moment it was impossible for her to guess how much of it was jest and what
proportion was earnest… It would have been unacceptable and annoying."

**WHAT**: Interiority is reported as sustained analytic summary of a character's
uncertainty; the corpus reports interior states as a plain declaration or through an
external comic particular, and does not sustain the analysis.

**CORPUS EVIDENCE**: sample-06 lines 105–107: "But deep below our freckles and
hay-coloured hair the unhandsomest of us dream of a prince or a princess" — one
sentence, then back to action. sample-08 lines 37–40: "Many a happy hour she had spent
planning for something nice for him." sample-02 lines 159–163: "Dulcie forgot everything
else for a moment except that she was beautiful." sample-07 lines 100–105 gives Maggie's
humiliation as Anna's elbow joggling Jimmy, not as analysis.

**CONFIDENCE**: low — reported because high-confidence findings sit throughout the same
draft.

---

**Categories assessed**

- 1 Register breaks — flagged (whole-document, above).
- 2 Constructions the author does not use — flagged (reader address; narratorial
  intrusion; vernacular dialogue).
- 3 Vocabulary reaching outside the corpus — clean. The draft's ornate words
  ("languorous", "befurbelowed", "sonorous") are within a range the corpus reaches
  ("persiflage", "pulchritude", "corniform", "terpsichorean"), and ornateness alone is
  not evidence.
- 4 Rhythm — not assessed; no deterministic scan was supplied.
- 5 Voice that never shifts — flagged; the corpus shifts visibly, so the test applies.
