# Voice review — case-35

No voice card supplied. Corpus present (10 samples), so the review proceeds. No
deterministic rhythm scan was supplied; category 4 is therefore not assessed rather than
guessed at.

## Findings

**LOCATION**: draft.txt L1–6 — "They formed a congenial group sitting there that summer
afternoon—Madame Ratignolle sewing away, often stopping to relate a story or incident with
much expressive gesture of her perfect hands; Robert and Mrs. Pontellier sitting idle,
exchanging occasional words, glances or smiles which indicated a certain advanced stage of
intimacy and _camaraderie_."
**WHAT**: The draft opens in third-person past-tense literary narration at a distance the
corpus never occupies, and holds it for all 139 lines; every paragraph could be lifted out
and dropped into a different document.
**CORPUS EVIDENCE**: All ten samples open a piece in first person or in direct address, in
present-tense argument: "There's plenty of reasons to be skeptical of centrists who bemoan
'political polarization'" (sample-01 L21); "Here's an irony: the 'gig economy' is a
statistical black hole" (sample-02 L21); "It may strike you as weird, but lenders love to
lend money to poor people" (sample-06 L21); "Forget 'Don't be evil'; Google's true motto is
a form of vulgar spidermanism" (sample-10 L21). The author writes toward a reader he
addresses as "you," and he is present in the text as "I" (sample-03 L41: "I'm 55"). Neither
appears anywhere in the draft.
**CONFIDENCE**: high

**LOCATION**: draft.txt L65–66 — "Never had that lady seemed a more tempting subject than at
that moment"; L81 — "As gently she repulsed him"; L82–84 — "She could not but believe it to
be thoughtlessness on his part; yet that was no reason she should submit to it."
**WHAT**: Subject–verb inversion after a fronted negative or adverb, and the
"could not but + infinitive" construction, are sentence shapes absent from every sample.
**CORPUS EVIDENCE**: Absent from all ten samples. Where the author fronts an element for
emphasis he does it without inversion and in plain order: "Nowhere is this failure to plan
more vivid than in the age distribution of both chambers" (sample-03 L35) is the single
inverted construction in the corpus, and it is a copular "is," not a lexical verb; he
otherwise writes "The point being that risk mitigation is never risk elimination"
(sample-06 L47), "This is bullshit, and the European Court of Justice affirmed it"
(sample-04 L41). For the negated-obligation idea the corpus uses a flat declarative: "But it
doesn't have to be this way" (sample-04 L43), "you can't figure out if a use is 'fair' just
by asking these questions" (sample-08 L43).
**CONFIDENCE**: high

**LOCATION**: draft.txt L19–20 — "for whatever crumbs of sympathy and comfort she might be
pleased to vouchsafe"; L74–75 — "giving forth little ejaculatory expressions of appreciation
in French"; L38 — "she interjoined, with excessive naïveté"; L52 — "He never assumed this
seriocomic tone"; L103–105 — "the breeze soft and languorous that came up from the south,
charged with the seductive odor of the sea. Children freshly befurbelowed"; L84 — "She did
not remonstrate."
**WHAT**: "vouchsafe," "interjoined," "befurbelowed," "seriocomic," "languorous,"
"remonstrate," "ejaculatory" sit at a level of formality and archaism outside the author's
range, and the untranslated French ("_Par exemple!_", "_Blagueur—farceur—gros bête, va!_",
L31, L50) is presented without gloss, which the corpus never does with a foreign or
specialist term.
**CORPUS EVIDENCE**: The corpus register is contemporary, profane, and technical, and
unfamiliar terms are always defined on first use: "'risk premium': the riskier a loan is, the
more it costs the borrower" (sample-06 L23); "'de minimis' (from a longer Latin phrase that
translates as 'the law does not concern itself with trifles')" (sample-08 L57);
"Collateralization – backing loans with legally binding promises to surrender some asset if
you default" (sample-06 L45). Where the author reaches for a heightened word he pairs it with
a vulgar one — "a demented hairball of form-filling" (sample-05 L107), "digital arm-breakers"
(sample-06 L81), "sphincter-puckering legal letter" (sample-08 L91), "tell the cartel to fuck
off" (sample-01 L73). Nothing in ten samples is pitched where "vouchsafe" and "befurbelowed"
are pitched.
**CONFIDENCE**: high

**LOCATION**: draft.txt L1–139, whole document.
**WHAT**: The draft holds one tone — detached, decorous narration — from first line to last,
including at moments of emotional weight (L90–92, the sketch destroyed; L108–116, Madame
Ratignolle's faintness), where the corpus would visibly change gear.
**CORPUS EVIDENCE**: The corpus shifts audibly with subject matter. Sample-03 moves from
wry ("the Mad King makes daily announcements of new wars, tariffs, monuments," L27) to plain
and bare when the subject turns personal ("I'm 55… It could be me next," L41; "He was only
44. I still miss him, every day," L71). Sample-08 ends on two three-word paragraphs ("Life is
hard. / Read books." L113–115) after ten paragraphs of copyright doctrine. Sample-04 drops
from procedural instruction (L57–61, Reader Mode keystrokes) into moral address ("we have a
duty and obligation to the people we love," L97). The draft has no comparable gear change.
**CONFIDENCE**: high
