**LOCATION**: line 1, "Men in great place are thrice servants—servants of the sovereign or

**CORPUS EVIDENCE**: All ten samples open on the writer inside an event and stay there:

first-person pronoun anywhere in 101 lines. Absent from all ten samples is any passage of
this length with no narrator in it.
