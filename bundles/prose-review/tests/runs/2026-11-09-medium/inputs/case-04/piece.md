I kept a notebook for eleven years before I understood what it was for.
---
It was not for remembering. The entries I reread are the ones I already remember.
---
It was for slowing down. A sentence written by hand takes as long as it takes.
---
The first three are bad and you throw them away. Not literally: you keep them, and once a decade you open one and wince.
---
Nobody needs a particular pen. What matters is that the tool cannot be edited.
