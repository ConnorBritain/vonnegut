CLASS: claim-drift
ORIGINAL: line 9–10, "I only remember that Vvedensky and I for some reason roared / with laughter as we listened to you."
REVISION: "I only remember that Vvedensky and I for some reason burst out / with laughter as we listened to you."
WHAT THE READER LOSES: the manner and intensity of the reaction. "Roared" describes loud, sustained laughing; "burst out" describes only a sudden onset, with no claim about volume or duration. The reader can no longer tell that Chekhov characterized the laughter as boisterous rather than merely abrupt.
