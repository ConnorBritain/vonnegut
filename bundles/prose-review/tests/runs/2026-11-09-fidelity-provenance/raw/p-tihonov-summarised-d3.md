  **WHAT THE READER LOSES**: The birth year. "Around the middle of the century" could mean anything from about 1845 to 1860, so the reader can no longer work out his age.

- **CLASS**: dropped-fact
  **ORIGINAL**: L12–13, "I finished the course at Taganrog high school in 1879."
  **REVISION**: L13, "finished at the Taganrog high school"
  **WHAT THE READER LOSES**: The year he left school. This was the only fixed point between his birth and his degree.

- **CLASS**: dropped-fact
  **ORIGINAL**: L13–14, "In 1884 I took my degree in medicine at the University of Moscow."
  **REVISION**: L13–14, "and some years later took my degree in medicine at the University of Moscow"
  **WHAT THE READER LOSES**: The year of the medical degree. "Some years later" hangs off a school date that is now gone too, so the degree cannot be dated at all.

- **CLASS**: dropped-fact
  **ORIGINAL**: L14–15, "In 1888 I gained the Pushkin prize."
  **REVISION**: L14–15, "I won a literary prize not long after"
  **WHAT THE READER LOSES**: Both the year (1888) and which prize it was. The Pushkin prize is a named, identifiable honour; "a literary prize" is not. "Not long after" also squeezes a four-year gap into what reads as a short one.

- **CLASS**: dropped-fact

  **REVISION**: L15–16, "travelled to Sahalin across Siberia and back by sea"
  **WHAT THE READER LOSES**: The year of the Sahalin journey. The sentence now gives no date for it at all.

- **CLASS**: dropped-fact
  **ORIGINAL**: L16, "In 1891 I made a tour in Europe"
  **REVISION**: L16, "and the following year made a tour in Europe"
  **WHAT THE READER LOSES**: The year of the European tour. "The following year" points back to the Sahalin journey, which is now undated, so 1891 cannot be recovered.

- **CLASS**: claim-drift
  **ORIGINAL**: L17–18, "In 1892 I took part in an orgy in the company of V. A. Tihonov at a name-day party."
  **REVISION**: L17–18, "Last year I took part in an orgy in the company of V. A. Tihonov at a name-day party."
  **WHAT THE READER LOSES**: The letter is dated 1892 (L2), so "last year" now places the orgy in 1891. That contradicts the original, and also the opening paragraph, which describes the same party as recent. The scan cannot see this because "1892" still appears in the dateline.

- **CLASS**: dropped-fact
  **ORIGINAL**: L18, "I began writing in 1879."
  **REVISION**: L18–19, "I began writing while still at school."
  **WHAT THE READER LOSES**: The year he started writing. The original says it was the year he finished school. "While still at school" is vaguer and could mean any earlier year.

- **CLASS**: dropped-fact

The reader also loses the count, since "several" replaces four. The title "Stories" is a single word, which the scan does not extract, so it was lost without being flagged.
