**Findings**

- **CLASS**: dropped-fact
- **ORIGINAL**: lines 8–10, "The other exhibition buildings I saw only from the outside, as they were occupied by cavalry brought there in anticipation of disorders."
- **REVISION**: lines 8–9, "I saw the other exhibition buildings from the outside."
- **WHAT THE READER LOSES**: The reader no longer learns that cavalry occupied the exhibition buildings in expectation of unrest. Without that, the reader cannot see why he stayed outside, or how seriously the authorities took the threat of riots.

- **CLASS**: dropped-qualification
- **ORIGINAL**: line 9, "saw only from the outside"
- **REVISION**: line 9, "saw the other exhibition buildings from the outside"
- **WHAT THE READER LOSES**: Without "only", the reader cannot tell that he was kept out. The revision allows that he looked at the outsides and then went in.

- **CLASS**: dropped-qualification
- **ORIGINAL**: lines 12–13, "To disperse a big crowd a dozen policemen are sufficient here."
- **REVISION**: lines 11–12, "To disperse a big crowd a dozen policemen are sufficient."
- **WHAT THE READER LOSES**: The original says a dozen police are enough in Paris, an observation about French crowds compared with home. Without "here" it reads as a general rule about crowds anywhere.

- **CLASS**: dropped-fact
- **ORIGINAL**: lines 29–30, "couldn’t see half the pictures, thanks to my short sight."
- **REVISION**: lines 28–29, "couldn’t see half the pictures."
- **WHAT THE READER LOSES**: The reader loses the stated reason he could not see the pictures. The link to the broken pince-nez is now left to inference, and the gap could just as well be read as crowds or bad hanging.

- **CLASS**: claim-drift
- **ORIGINAL**: lines 31–32, "In comparison with the landscape painters I saw here yesterday Levitan is a king...."
- **REVISION**: line 30, "Levitan is a king...."
- **WHAT THE READER LOSES**: In the original, Levitan is a king next to the French landscape painters he saw the day before. In the revision he is simply a king, with no comparison, no genre and no occasion.

- **CLASS**: dropped-fact
- **ORIGINAL**: line 49, "down the riots in Fourmis (there were many killed and wounded)."
- **REVISION**: line 47, "down the riots in Fourmis."
- **WHAT THE READER LOSES**: The reader no longer learns that the suppression killed and wounded many people. That is the cause of the Minister being called to account, and it is why the sitting was stormy.
