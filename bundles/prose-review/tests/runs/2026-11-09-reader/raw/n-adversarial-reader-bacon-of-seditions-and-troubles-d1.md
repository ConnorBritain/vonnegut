**WHERE I STOPPED**: line 159–160, "an emblem, no doubt, to show how safe it is for monarchs to make sure of the good-will of common people."
**WHY, AS THIS READER**: A poets' story about Jupiter and Briareus is offered as the proof that monarchs are safe with the common people on their side, and "no doubt" covers for the fact that nothing in the fable shows it.
