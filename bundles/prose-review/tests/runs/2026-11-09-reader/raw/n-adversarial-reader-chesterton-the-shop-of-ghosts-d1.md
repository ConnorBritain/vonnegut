**WHY, AS THIS READER**: The whole case is that he has always been dying, so he never will. Everything that did finally die also spent a long time "always dying" first. The witnesses are Dickens, Steele, Jonson and Robin Hood, all of whom loved the custom, so they show only that people kept predicting its death, not that the prediction must keep failing.
