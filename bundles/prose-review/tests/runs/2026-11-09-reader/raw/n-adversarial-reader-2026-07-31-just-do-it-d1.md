**WHY, AS THIS READER**: The argument tests a use by whether anyone will find out, not by whether it is legitimate. Lines 101 and 111 admit the reader can't tell which uses are which and leave it there, so that is a concession and not an answer.

**FORCED CHOICE**: "There is a whole universe of uses that might technically violate copyright, might technically not fit into de minimis, first sale or fair use – but these are also uses that no one would ever find out." This sentence does the most work in the second half. It moves the essay from "you don't need permission for lawful uses" to "you don't need permission for unlawful ones either", and its only support is that nobody will catch you.
