**WHERE I STOPPED**: lines 29–30, "You can find there (as commonly happens in modern centres) the worst things of all nations--the _Daily Mail_ from England, the cheap philosophies from Germany, the loose novels of France, and the drinks of America."
**WHY, AS THIS READER**: The parenthesis admits that every modern centre has this list, and Paris is a modern centre, so the evidence meant to show Brussels as the worse city fits Paris just as well. That leaves the "bad Paris" charge resting on assertion alone.

**FORCED CHOICE**: lines 3–5, "No one can understand Paris and its history who does not understand that its fierceness is the balance and justification of its frivolity."
**WHY**: The whole case against Brussels depends on this claim, and the piece never argues it. It just states that self-inflicted pain excuses indecency, and you only accept that if you already believe it.
