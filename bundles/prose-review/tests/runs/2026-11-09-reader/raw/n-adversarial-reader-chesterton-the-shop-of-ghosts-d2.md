**FORCED CHOICE**: line 182, "I understand it now," he cried, "you will never die."

This is the line I would stand up at. The whole piece builds to it, and it rests on one inference: people in every age said Christmas was dying, so it never will. That doesn't follow. A thing can be mourned too early many times and still die in the end, and every witness the story calls is a writer who loved Christmas.

**Nothing here for**: the strongest objection the piece never answers, the weakest link in the argument, the claim that does the most work with the least support, the example that proves less than it is asked to. All four point at the last line, and the piece ends on it, so it gives me no place to put the draft down early.
