ORIGINAL: line 18, "In 1888 I took the Pushkin prize."
REVISION: "The Pushkin prize came in 1888."
WHAT THE READER LOSES: The original casts Chekhov as the actor who took the prize; the revision makes the prize itself the subject, an event that arrived rather than something he did. The reader can no longer tell whether Chekhov is describing an achievement he pursued or an honor that simply occurred to him — a change in agency the words alone carry, not in what happened.
