# CLAUDE.md snippet

**Most people need nothing here.** The skill is dispatcher-triggered — "add
this source", "which of these claims have I got a source for", "check the
quotes in this piece" reach it from its description.

Add the block below only if you have the specific problem it solves:
**researched pieces ship without their quotes being checked against the
sources.**

---

```markdown
## Prose research   <!-- vonnegut: prose-research -->

Before I call a researched piece done, run `prose-research`'s claims-check
over it with a sentence map, and quote every drifted quote, dead link and
unledgered claim as the tool prints them. Never tell me a claim is true;
tell me whether it is ledgered and whether the quote is exact. Save nothing
to the dossier or ledger unless I say so.
```
