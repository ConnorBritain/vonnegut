## Current deterministic foundation

The new `visible-prose/1` normalizer and `voice-profile/3` tooling are separate
from the historical measurement/scoring implementation. Existing artifacts are
not rewritten. Current evidence is generated by `v040-measurements.mjs --update`
and reproduced in check mode and in the ordinary author selftest.

| Corpus | Pieces | Author-visible words | Round parenthetical spans |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Doctorow | 10 | 17,685 | 97 |
| EFF Mullin | 11 | 8,233 | 11 |

The historical Mullin counter reported 97. The current count excludes link
destinations and retains two genuine asides containing links. A span is a
surface-form count, not proof of its rhetorical function.

Leave-one-out diagnostics compare each piece to other pieces' observed min/max
rates within the same metadata group. At least one departure occurs in 8/10
Doctorow pieces and 7/11 Mullin pieces (17 and 11 individual departures).
These are descriptive departures by real human writing, not failures of those
writers. They are evidence against imposing every observed range as a hard rule,
not a statistical claim that these ranges are calibrated prediction intervals.

Explicit phrase, punctuation, length and enumerated-counter rules operate
independently of corpus observations. Empty denominators are `not-evaluated`;
explicit zeroes have no one-hit exception. Counts use actual final draft text.

  command, with executable-reference fixtures and concise conversational receipts.

  immutable diffs, and one-active-preference comparisons without saving changes.
- Examples-only jobs can explicitly skip profile rendering. Profile preparation
  receives cancellation and has structured call records; invocation accounting
  includes preparation as well as draft/review calls.
- Current primitive prompts, rendered agents and self-contained skill prompt
  copies now share byte-identical bodies. `render-current-prompts.mjs` owns the
  two deployments. Models inherit configuration instead of hard-coding Sonnet.
- Repair inputs retain the same profile, examples and rules. Voice review
  accounts for every supported observation, sees descriptive deviations, and
  can compare a repair with the previous candidate without treating it as corpus.
- The quota-era source prompts and acceptance runner are archived as exact v0.3
  fixtures. Historical scoring is unchanged; its canary runs in a temporary
  layout containing hash-verified original inputs, without changing raw evidence.
