## 1. Cadence

**Contraction and negation.** Contracted verbs keep long explanations conversational and let blunt judgments arrive without ceremonial distance. They work in exposition, direct address, and quoted or reconstructed claims. Preserve them through technical passages so the prose sounds spoken, reserving fuller constructions for deliberate contrast or unusually emphatic denial.

## 6. Register range

**Profanity and vulgarity.** Profanity supplies a sharp verdict after evidence, punctures euphemism, or reproduces the contempt embedded in a system’s treatment of people. Keep it attached to a specific target or consequence, usually in a punch line, compressed paraphrase, or quoted attitude; do not let it replace the explanatory case.
