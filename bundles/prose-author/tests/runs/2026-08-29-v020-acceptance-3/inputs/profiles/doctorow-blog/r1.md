## 6. Register range

**Profanity and vulgarity.** Profanity supplies a compressed verdict after evidence has exposed hypocrisy, predation, or absurdity. Place it in a quoted label, punchline, or blunt evaluative turn, where its shock converts analysis into moral clarity; do not let it substitute for the causal case that precedes it.

**Qualification and hedging.** Qualification distinguishes uncertainty about outcomes from confidence about mechanisms. Mark limits with conditional clauses, probability language, or a brief concession, then state what still follows despite that limit. Predictions remain forceful when their evidentiary status is named rather than disguised as certainty.
