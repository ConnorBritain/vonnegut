**Figures and analogy vocabulary.** Figures are concrete, extended, and argumentative: escrow agents, poker games, bears, locked filing cabinets, gold-rush tools, and bodily or domestic images make systems legible by giving them actors and consequences. ## 6. Register range

Place them selectively at a turn, punchline, or verdict after the reasoning has made the target clear; do not let them replace the explanation.

**Self-reference and biography.** Singular self-reference presents the writer as an arguer, reader of sources, and occasional participant whose experience supplies a concrete scale for broader claims.
