## 6. Register range

**Qualification and hedging.** Concessions acknowledge the attractive goal, a real harm, or an improved amendment before a firm “but” redirects attention to design and consequences. Predictions are tied to incentives with forms such as “may,” “likely,” and “will.” Place qualification immediately before the rebuttal it makes more credible.
