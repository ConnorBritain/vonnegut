# Where this plan came from

**Hand-authored. This is the seam S4 does not automate.**

The four entries come from one voice-critic finding on the S3 `matched-chekhov` draft —
draw 1, high confidence:

> The draft contains no contracted forms anywhere in its ~490 words, using the full form
> at every opportunity, including in casual asides where the author's letters contract by
> default. […] Nine of the ten samples contract, and the ones closest to this draft in
> register do it densely.

That finding is unusual in being **consolidatable at all**: it happens to cite four exact
spans, and each has one obvious substitution. Most voice findings do not. The same round's
other finding — *"a paragraph-length held stillness where the corpus's descriptions are
rapid and interruptive"* — names a whole paragraph and no specific edit, and a surgical
reviser has nothing to act on. It is deliberately **not** in this plan.

That asymmetry is the argument for FU-2. A human picked which finding could become a plan
and which could not, and that judgement is the consolidator.

**What the loop harness does here:** `nextAction` returns `consolidate` and stops. It does
not guess.
