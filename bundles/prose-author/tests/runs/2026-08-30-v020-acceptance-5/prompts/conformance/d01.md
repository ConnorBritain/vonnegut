- o02 [person-reader-stance; section:address; measurement:first-person-plural-family]: First-person plural language builds a shared public position with the returning readership, often grouping writer and reader as people exposed to the same systems or capable of collective action. - o08 [interruption-punctuation; section:cadence; measurement:round-parenthetical-spans]: Parentheses carry compact asides, qualifications, translations, dates, technical clarifications, and comic afterthoughts without interrupting the main syntactic line.
