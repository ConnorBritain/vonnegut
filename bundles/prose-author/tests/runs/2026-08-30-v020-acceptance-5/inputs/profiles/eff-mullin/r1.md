## 6. Register range

**Qualification and hedging.** Qualification usually appears through explicit concessions, conditional predictions, and carefully bounded claims: a proposal may address one concern while leaving others intact, or a result may be likely rather than certain. Place the concession before the decisive criticism, then distinguish documented effects from forecasts and unresolved legal questions.
