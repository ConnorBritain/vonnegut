# The amended rule, as the render applied it

## Section 5 — both halves present

> The figures are assembled out of hyphenated compounds and coinages, and that unit *is*
> countable. Counting tokens matching lowercase-hyphen-lowercase, two or more letters each
> side: **168 instances, 9.65 per 1,000 words — 10/10 samples, throughout**. This is not a
> count of figures. It is a count of the unit they are built from, under the rule just
> stated — **use hyphenated compounds at about this rate.**

> A second countable component: the body and its excretions supply the vocabulary of
> contempt. Counting the list *orifice, orifices, piss, piss-bottles, pee, bladder,
> bladders, balls, vagina, vaginas, asshole, assholes, kidney, kidneys, gut, guts, flora,
> throat, stomach, blood, bodies, body*: **20 instances, 1.15 per 1,000 words — 7/10
> samples, several times per piece**. **Use that vocabulary at about this rate.**

The disclaimer is there. So is the instruction. The previous render wrote *"a drafter
should read them as raw material rather than as a target"* and the draft used the
vocabulary zero times.

## It also kept the honest refusal where it belonged

> No rate: I cannot state a rule for where a figure starts and stops that a stranger would
> apply the same way.

> No rate: I cannot bound "a judgement" reproducibly.

> No rate: I cannot write a rule for "the term the piece has been contesting" that a
> stranger reproduces from the text alone.

Three refusals, each naming the reason. The rule did not turn into "rate everything".

## And it derived FU-14's finding independently, with numbers

> The short sentence is a paragraph *opener*, not a paragraph closer... Only 24% of short
> sentences end their paragraph, against a 40.5% baseline for all 733 sentences — so if you
> want the flat verdict, **put it at the top of the paragraph and unpack it underneath, not
> at the bottom as a punch.**

FU-14 took six critic complaints and three failed fixes to reach that conclusion. The
renderer reached it from the corpus, quantified it, and wrote it as a drafting instruction.
