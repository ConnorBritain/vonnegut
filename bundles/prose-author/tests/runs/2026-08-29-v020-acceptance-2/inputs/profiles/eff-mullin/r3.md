## 3. How a piece closes

Section endings crystallize a consequence in a short declarative line. ## 4. Who is being addressed, and how

**Person, number, and reader stance.** Direct address enters chiefly when the analysis turns into a practical invitation or asks readers to test a legal claim against ordinary experience. Keep “you” localized to calls to contact lawmakers or compact thought experiments; let the policy explanation otherwise speak about users, families, developers, or the public.

## 5. Figures

Use a brief image after the technical setup to clarify scale, incentives, or danger; return promptly to literal policy analysis.

## 6. Register range

**Qualification and hedging.** Concessions acknowledge a legitimate aim or a narrowed amendment before separating it from the mechanism’s remaining harms: transparency is “laudable,” concerns are real, and revisions matter, but consequences persist. Put the concession near the opening or section pivot, then qualify predictions with practical incentives, likelihood, or unresolved margins.
