## 5. Figures

**Figures and analogy vocabulary.** Draw figures from familiar systems—card catalogs, vaults, drag-nets, movies, and impossible compliance tasks—to translate institutional machinery into visible consequences. Keep each comparison compact, then return immediately to the policy mechanism it clarifies. Let the image simplify scale, risk, or incentives rather than decorate the prose.

## 6. Register range

**Qualification and hedging.** Concede a proposal’s legitimate aim or acknowledge an amendment before identifying what remains defective. Mark uncertainty with bounded legal formulations—“could be read,” “likely,” “at a minimum”—when predicting enforcement or interpreting reach, but state documented mechanisms and direct textual contradictions without cushioning them.
