- o07 [interruption-punctuation; section:cadence; measurement:round-parenthetical-spans]: Parentheses insert compact qualifications, definitions, examples, and source-oriented clarifications without interrupting the main argumentative line. - o13 [openings-endings-closure; section:openings]: Openings commonly name the bill or practice, state its official purpose, and pivot quickly to the concrete harm or public-interest conflict.
