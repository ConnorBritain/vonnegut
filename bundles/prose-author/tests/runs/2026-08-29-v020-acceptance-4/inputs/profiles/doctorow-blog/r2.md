## 6. Register range

**Profanity and vulgarity.** Profanity supplies compressed moral judgment, ridicules euphemism, and marks the point where a technical explanation cashes out as abuse or absurdity. Place it at a reveal, punch line, or quoted characterization; keep it semantically pointed rather than using it as ambient intensification.

**Self-reference and biography.** The individual voice appears as witness, reader, practitioner, or arguer: it recalls an encounter, discloses a relevant stake, and states where an interpretation diverges from a source. Use first-person material to establish argumentative standing or bridge abstraction to experience, not as detached autobiography.

**Qualification and hedging.** Qualification distinguishes empirical limits from rhetorical certainty: concessions such as “of course,” conditional if-then sequences, and explicit scope markers grant a premise before narrowing or reversing it. Place the caveat just before the stronger causal claim so restraint strengthens, rather than blurs, the conclusion.
