## 6. Register range

**Profanity and vulgarity.** Profanity supplies compressed moral judgment, punctures euphemism, and makes an exploitative arrangement sound as indecent as the argument says it is. Place it at a reveal, punchline, quoted characterization, or decisive dismissal; do not let it replace the preceding causal explanation.
