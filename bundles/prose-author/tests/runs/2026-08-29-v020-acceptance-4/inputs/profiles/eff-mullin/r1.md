## 6. Register range

**Qualification and hedging.** Concessions acknowledge a legitimate goal, a narrowed definition, or a real underlying concern before showing why the remedy still fails. Predictions are tied to incentives with modals such as “may,” “could,” and “will likely.” Place qualification before the rebuttal so criticism appears bounded by evidence rather than indiscriminate.
