- o07 [interruption-punctuation; section:cadence; measurement:round-parenthetical-spans]: Parentheses insert compact identifiers, age definitions, examples, or clarifying equivalents without diverting the main claim. - o12 [figures-analogy; section:figures]: Figures draw from ordinary systems and physical consequences: a card catalog instead of a database, a valuable vault, a dragnet, a smokescreen, or having “seen this movie before.” Use a short comparison after technical explanation to make administrative failure or legal incentives tangible, then return promptly to literal consequences.
- o13 [openings-endings-closure; section:closings]: Openings name the measure or event, summarize its stated purpose, and quickly pivot to the concealed cost.
