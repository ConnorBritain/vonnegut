## 6. Register range

**Profanity and vulgarity.** Vulgar language supplies comic abrasion and moral force, particularly when exposing hypocrisy, coercion, incompetence, or bodily indignity. It tends to land after a technical or institutional setup, making the judgment memorable rather than ornamental. Place it at a pressure point or punchline, and keep surrounding explanation precise.
