- o06 [questions-imperatives-vocatives; section:address; measurement:question-marks]: Questions usually expose an operational gap by asking how a platform, family, or business could satisfy the proposal in practice. - o08 [interruption-punctuation; section:cadence; measurement:em-dashes]: Em dashes sharpen pivots, attach an emphatic consequence, or interrupt a general claim with a decisive specification. - o09 [interruption-punctuation; section:absences; measurement:en-dashes]: Interruption and qualification are carried by em dashes or parentheses, while the en dash does not provide a stable rhetorical device across the corpus. - o12 [figures-analogy; section:figures]: Concrete figures translate infrastructure and liability into familiar objects or motion: a registry becomes a “card catalog,” stored conversations a “valuable vault,” and speech restrictions a “dragnet.” Extend the comparison only far enough to clarify scale, incentive, or danger, usually at the end of an explanatory passage.
- o13 [openings-endings-closure; section:closings]: Openings name the live bill or development, summarize its advertised purpose, and pivot quickly to the central defect.
