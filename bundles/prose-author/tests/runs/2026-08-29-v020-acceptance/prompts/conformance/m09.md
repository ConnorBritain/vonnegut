- o07 [interruption-punctuation; section:cadence; measurement:round-parenthetical-spans]: Parentheses efficiently decode acronyms, supply legal examples, identify age ranges, or add a tightly bounded qualification without derailing the main claim. - o08 [interruption-punctuation; section:cadence; measurement:em-dashes]: Em dashes sharpen pivots between an official description and the practical reality, insert consequential examples, or land a corrective phrase after a complete claim. - o09 [interruption-punctuation; section:absences; measurement:en-dashes]: Use the established em dash or a parenthetical span for pivots, examples, and qualifications rather than relying on an en dash as an interruption mark. - o13 [openings-endings-closure; section:closings]: Open with the live legislative or technological development, identify its advertised purpose, then pivot quickly to the concealed practical problem.
