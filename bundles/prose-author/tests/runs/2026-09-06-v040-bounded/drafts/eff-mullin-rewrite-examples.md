This comment concerns a fictional scenario, not a real library program.

The town library hasn’t signed the contract for its proposed six-month reading-app pilot. That leaves time to change a clause permitting the vendor to keep identifiable reading histories for two years after a patron closes an account.

I oppose that clause. Keeping those records could discourage some patrons from exploring subjects privately—although I can’t say whether that would happen here.

The board should ask the vendor to retain only anonymous aggregate usage totals and let patrons read without creating an account. Those changes would make the pilot more acceptable to me.

The board accepts written comments until June 30. I would like it to consider this alternative before signing the contract.
