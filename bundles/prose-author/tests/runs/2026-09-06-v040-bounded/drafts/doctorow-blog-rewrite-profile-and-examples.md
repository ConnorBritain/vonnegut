(A fictional scenario for a writing exercise.)

The town library hasn't signed the contract for its proposed six-month reading-app pilot. There's still time to change the terms. The vendor's proposed clause permits keeping identifiable reading histories for two years after a patron closes an account – two years after that patron leaves.

I oppose that clause. Keeping those records could discourage some patrons from exploring subjects privately, although I can't say whether that would happen here. That uncertainty doesn't change my objection.

The board should ask the vendor to retain only anonymous aggregate usage totals and let patrons read without creating an account. Those changes would make the pilot more acceptable to me.

The board accepts written comments until June 30. I'd like it to consider this alternative while the contract is still unsigned.
