In this fictional scenario, the town library hasn’t signed the contract for its proposed six-month reading-app pilot. There’s still time to change its terms. The vendor’s proposed contract permits retaining identifiable reading histories for two years after a patron closes an account.

I oppose that clause. Keeping those records could discourage some patrons from exploring subjects privately—although I cannot say whether that would happen here.

The board should ask the vendor to retain only anonymous aggregate usage totals and let patrons read without creating an account. Those changes would make the pilot more acceptable to me.

The library board accepts written comments until June 30. I would like the board to consider this alternative before signing the contract.
