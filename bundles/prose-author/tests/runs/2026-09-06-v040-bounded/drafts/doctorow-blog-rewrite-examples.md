(A fictional library-board comment for a writing exercise.)

The town library hasn't signed the contract for its proposed six-month reading-app pilot. There's still time to change the terms. The vendor's proposed contract would allow it to keep identifiable reading histories for two years after a patron closes an account. Close the account, and the reading history can stay.

I oppose that clause. Keeping those records could discourage some patrons from exploring subjects privately (though I can't say whether that would happen here).

The board should ask the vendor for two changes: retain only anonymous aggregate usage totals, and let patrons read without creating an account. Those changes would make the pilot more acceptable to me.

The board accepts written comments until June 30. I'd like the board to consider this alternative before signing the contract.
