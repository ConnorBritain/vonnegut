In this fictional scenario, the town library is considering a six-month reading-app pilot. The proposed contract would let the vendor retain identifiable reading histories for two years after a patron closes an account – a long tail for a short trial.

The library hasn't signed. There's still time to change that clause, and I oppose it. Keeping those records could discourage some patrons from exploring subjects privately, although I can't say whether that would happen here.

The board should ask the vendor to retain only anonymous aggregate usage totals and let patrons read without creating an account. These changes would make the pilot more acceptable to me.

The board accepts written comments until June 30. I'd like it to consider this alternative before signing, while the terms can still be changed.
