Peter Oloche David (also published as Peter O. David and Peter Oloche O. David ) is a Nigerian researcher, entrepreneur, and company executive. He is recognized for contributions to biomedical and computational research and is profiled in professional and institutional networks.

David's research contributions focus on biomedical data analysis and computational approaches in cancer research. He has co-authored abstracts presented at international conferences, including AACR special sessions on synthetic vulnerabilities and pediatric cancer research.
