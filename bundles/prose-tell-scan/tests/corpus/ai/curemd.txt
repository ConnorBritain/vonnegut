CureMD

CureMD is a healthcare technology company based in the United States, known for offering cloud-based electronic health record (EHR), practice management, medical billing, and patient engagement solutions for healthcare providers. The company serves a broad range of medical practices and healthcare organizations, with a focus on simplifying clinical, administrative, and financial operations.

History

CureMD was founded in 1997 with the mission of modernizing healthcare delivery through technology. Since its inception, the company has developed integrated health information systems designed to improve workflow efficiency, patient care, and compliance with regulatory standards.

In recent years, CureMD has expanded its offerings to include value-based care support, population health tools, and AI-driven documentation features. The company supports tens of thousands of providers across the United States.

Products and Services

Electronic Health Records (EHR)

CureMD’s cloud-based EHR system is certified and designed for customizable charting, ePrescribing, decision support, lab connectivity, and reporting. It supports over 32 medical specialties and aims to reduce documentation time and enhance clinical accuracy.

Practice Management and Medical Billing

CureMD provides a revenue cycle management (RCM) platform that includes appointment scheduling, insurance verification, claim submission, denial management, and payment posting. The company was recognized by KLAS Research as one of the top-performing RCM vendors. 1

Patient Portal and Telehealth

The patient portal allows for secure messaging, appointment requests, access to medical records, and bill payments. The platform also offers integrated telehealth functionality, enabling virtual visits directly from the CureMD interface.

AI Scribe

In 2025, CureMD launched AI Scribe , an ambient documentation and order-entry assistant that captures natural patient-provider conversations and generates structured clinical notes in real time. The tool aims to reduce provider burnout by automating clinical documentation tasks. 2

Partnerships and Integrations

CureMD has engaged in several strategic partnerships to enhance its service offerings:

In 2024, it partnered with i2i Population Health to improve data-driven care coordination and population health management. 3

The same year, CureMD integrated with Stitch PEO to offer HR and payroll services tailored to medical practices. 4

In 2023, CureMD collaborated with Tempus to enable genomic testing integration for oncology decision support within its EHR platform. 5


Certifications and Awards

CureMD received EHNAC Accreditation in 2024, affirming its adherence to rigorous data privacy, security, and operational standards. 6

It has been recognized by industry publications like KLAS Research , TechRadar , and Forbes Advisor for its user-friendly interface, comprehensive features, and practice performance optimization capabilities. 17


Reception and Recognition

In independent reviews, CureMD has been described as a versatile and scalable solution for small to medium-sized practices. A 2024 TechRadar review noted its ease of use, robust billing module, and multi-specialty support. 7 Forbes Advisor also highlighted CureMD’s value proposition in the EHR software landscape. 8
