Aleftina Nikolaevna Evdokimova (Russian: Алевтина Николаевна Евдокимова ; 6 October 1939 – 30 April 2025) was a Soviet and Russian actress of film and stage. She was best known for her roles in Courier (1986), Let the Charms Last Long (1984), and Ekipazh (1980). A member of the Maly Theatre from 1963, she was honored as People's Artist of the RSFSR in 1992.
