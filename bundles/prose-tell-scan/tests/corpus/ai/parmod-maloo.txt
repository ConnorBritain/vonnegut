Pramod Maloo (born 19 December 1982) is an Indian entrepreneur, author, speaker, and digital marketing professional[cite: 2, 3]. He is the founder of Kreative Machinez , a digital marketing agency based in India, and the author of The Start-Up Founder’s Guide to Digital Marketing , published by HarperCollins India [cite: 4, 38]. His professional work spans digital marketing strategy, agency operations, entrepreneurship education, and public speaking[cite: 5].


Early life and education



Maloo completed his Bachelor of Commerce degree from The Bhawanipur Education Society College, Kolkata, between 2001 and 2004[cite: 7]. He later pursued a Master of Business Administration (MBA) in Marketing from IBS Hyderabad , graduating in 2008[cite: 7]. During his postgraduate studies, he worked at NetElixir Pvt. Ltd. as a PPC Expert from November 2007 to February 2008[cite: 8].

Career



Early career



Maloo’s early career involved hands-on roles in digital advertising during the formative years of India’s digital services industry[cite: 12]. In 2008, he worked as a Digital Consultant at Quasar Media Pvt. Ltd. in New Delhi, where he managed banner advertising, SEO, and pay-per-click campaigns[cite: 13, 14].

Kreative Machinez



Maloo founded Kreative Machinez in February 2010[cite: 17]. The agency provides services in search engine marketing, social media marketing, web development, and online branding[cite: 18]. The company employs more than 100 people and has worked with over 1,000 client accounts across sectors such as healthcare, real estate, and e-commerce[cite: 19, 20, 21].

Other ventures



Maloo has been involved in several initiatives across technology and fintech:

Web Scholarz (2012–2015) : Founded a digital marketing training initiative[cite: 28].

Edventure Online Pvt. Ltd. (2012–present) : Serves as Director of Technology for an education guidance company[cite: 29].

Yes She Will (2020–2025) : Co-founded a global initiative supporting women entrepreneurs[cite: 33].

SHB Fintech Pvt. Ltd. (2023–present) : Co-founded a fintech startup operating under the name Sahi Hai Bazaar[cite: 35].

PraMotivation (2019–present) : Founded a platform focused on motivational content and personal development[cite: 36].


Books and publications



Maloo is the author of The Start-Up Founder’s Guide to Digital Marketing [cite: 38]. The book outlines marketing concepts relevant to startup founders and has appeared on Amazon India’s bestseller lists in the marketing category[cite: 39, 40]. It is also part of the library collection of the Ahmedabad Management Association (AMA)[cite: 41].

Public speaking and media



Maloo has been a speaker at the IIT Delhi Conclave and conducted a digital marketing workshop at the Global Entrepreneurship Summit (GES ’26) organised by IIT Kharagpur [cite: 45, 46]. In December 2024, he provided commentary to Exchange4Media on quick commerce advertising[cite: 49]. He has also been featured in Business World [cite: 52].
