Fictional character from Chainsaw Man




Reze (レゼ) is a fictional character in the Japanese manga series Chainsaw Man by Tatsuki Fujimoto . She appears as a central character and also the main antagonist in the Bomb Girl arc, where she initially befriends protagonist Denji before revealing her identity as a hybrid of the Bomb Devil, tasked with capturing his heart. Bomb Girl arc was adapted into the 2025 anime film Chainsaw Man – The Movie: Reze Arc , in which she is voiced by Reina Uedain the original Japanese version and Alexis Tipton in the English dub.0


Character concept and creation



Reze was created by Tatsuki Fujimoto as part of the Bomb Girl arc in Chainsaw Man , an early story arc in the series that explores Denji’s vulnerability and emotional development through a relationship that intertwines affection and deception. Her character blends elements of romance, tragedy, and violence, contrasting with Denji’s straightforward motivations and driving much of the narrative’s dramatic tension.

Her dual role as both a charismatic romantic interest and a deadly antagonist highlights a recurring theme in the series: the juxtaposition of human vulnerability and destructive power. Reze’s design and narrative role emphasize her function as a foil to Denji, challenging his naivety and emotional resilience.

Appearances



Manga



Reze first appears in Chainsaw Man chapter 40 as a mysterious woman who rapidly forms a bond with Denji. Their relationship deepens over a series of encounters, during which Reze’s true identity as a Soviet-trained Bomb Devil hybrid is revealed. Her mission is to capture the Chainsaw Devil’s heart for her handlers, leading to violent confrontations with Denji and other characters. The arc plays out as both a personal tragedy and a high-stakes battle, with Reze’s dual motivations and ambiguous feelings toward Denji contributing to the story’s emotional impact.

Anime film



In the 2025 anime adaptation titled Chainsaw Man – The Movie: Reze Arc , which adapts the Bomb Girl story, Reze’s role is expanded and dramatized for the big screen. The film preserves her narrative arc, showcasing her complex relationship with Denji and the explosive confrontations that define her character’s arc. The movie’s marketing and critical reception highlighted the dynamic between Reze and Denji, emphasizing both the emotional and action-oriented elements of her story.1

Voice actors



**Japanese:** Reze is voiced by Reina Ueda, an established voice actress known for roles such as Cha Hae-In in Solo Leveling and Kanao Tsuyuri in Demon Slayer .2

**English:** In the English dub of the anime film, Reze is voiced by Alexis Tipton, noted for her work in series such as Kaguya-sama: Love is War .3


Reception



Critical and audience response to Reze’s appearance and the film adaptation of her arc has been generally positive, especially regarding the emotional depth of her relationship with Denji and the way the adaptation handles their dynamic. *CBR* praised the film’s ability to bring new life to Reze’s interactions with Denji and capture nuanced performances from the voice cast, noting how the movie’s cinematography and voice acting enhanced key emotional scenes.4

The film itself holds a high rating on **Rotten Tomatoes** and has been described as a major anime release of 2025, indicating strong overall reception for the Reze Arc storyline and its adaptation.5

However, some individual reviewers have expressed mixed opinions about the adaptation’s pacing and handling of Reze’s character, arguing that certain elements, such as romantic or deeper characterization scenes, might lean more toward fan service than narrative exploration. These criticisms highlight differing views among audiences over how the source material was translated to screen.6

Powers and abilities



As a Bomb Devil hybrid, Reze is highly agile and capable of generating explosive attacks. In combat she demonstrates both strategic intelligence and brute force, creating and deploying explosive devices and transforming parts of her body into powerful weapons. Her hybrid form gives her enhanced durability and regeneration, traits typical of human-devil hybrids in the series.7

Cultural impact



Reze has become a memorable figure among Chainsaw Man fans, particularly following the release of the anime film. Fans and critics alike often discuss the emotional complexity of her arc and its impact on Denji’s development, with many polls and discussions citing her role as one of the most poignant in the early part of the series. In official Shonen Jump popularity polls, she has consistently ranked among the more popular characters from the franchise.8
