Shopping mall in West Delhi, India


Pacific Mall, Tagore Garden is a shopping mall located in Tagore Garden , West Delhi , India , near the Subhash Nagar metro station on the Delhi Metro Blue Line . It is developed and operated by Pacific Development Corporation Private Limited. The mall opened on 21 January 2011.


History



Pacific Mall, Tagore Garden opened on 21 January 2011 on Najafgarh Road in West Delhi. The mall was developed by Pacific Development Corporation Private Limited as an organised retail destination serving West Delhi and surrounding areas.

In 2021, the mall completed ten years of operations. During this period it expanded its tenant mix to include international fashion brands alongside food, beverage and entertainment facilities.

In January 2026, the mall completed 15 years of operations. According to trade publications, the mall had by this point transitioned from a conventional shopping centre to a retail destination combining shopping, leisure and community engagement, supported by anchor-led leasing and a mix of domestic and international brands.

Location and accessibility



The mall is situated on Najafgarh Road, Tagore Garden, New Delhi – 110018. It is accessible via the Subhash Nagar metro station on the Delhi Metro Blue Line , serving residential areas including Rajouri Garden , Punjabi Bagh , Vikaspuri , and Paschim Vihar .

The mall operates daily from 11:00 AM to 12:00 AM.

Layout



The mall has a built-up area of 6,78,000 sq. ft., a gross leasable area (GLA) of 5,06,000 sq. ft., a carpet area of 4,06,000 sq. ft., and a common area of 72,000 sq. ft. It houses 148 stores and 50 kiosks across five floors.


Parking



The mall provides multi-level parking with a capacity of 1,210 four-wheeler spaces and 600 two-wheeler spaces, along with electric vehicle charging and valet parking.

Anchor tenants




IKEA



In August 2025, IKEA opened a 15,000 sq. ft. store at Pacific Mall, Tagore Garden, marking the retailer's first location in Delhi and in North India.

PVR Cinemas



The mall houses a PVR Cinemas multiplex with 6 screens and 1,088 seats across 49,000 sq. ft. In 2026, the multiplex was renovated with upgraded projection and sound systems. Ticket prices range from ₹149 to ₹1,500.

Tenants



The mall houses over 300 brands across fashion, lifestyle, electronics, food and beverage, and home furnishing.

Notable fashion tenants include Zara , Bershka , Uniqlo , H&M , and OVS . Bershka, part of the Inditex Group, opened its first Delhi NCR store at the mall.

Other tenants include IKEA , Dyson , Marks & Spencer , Decathlon , Nike , Adidas , Lacoste , Tommy Hilfiger , Calvin Klein , New Balance , Onitsuka Tiger , Charles & Keith , Hidesign , and Armani .

Food and beverage



The mall's food court, Salt, spans 25,000 sq. ft. with a seating capacity of approximately 1,000 across 30 outlets. Brands present include KFC , Subway , Burger King , Haldiram's , Pizza Hut , and Domino's .

Sit-down restaurants at the mall include Chili's , Daryaganj, Sagar Ratna , and Cafeteria & Co.

Entertainment



The mall's entertainment facilities include:

PVR Cinemas – 6-screen multiplex, 49,000 sq. ft., 1,088 seats

Time Zone – Family Entertainment Centre, 8,500 sq. ft.

Funky Kingdom – Family Entertainment Centre, 7,500 sq. ft.




Events



The mall has hosted public events including the Mela Mela Anime Japan 2025 festival, featuring anime screenings and concerts. The Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO) participated in the 2025 edition of the event.

Footfall



According to the Shopping Centres Association of India, the mall receives approximately 35,000 visitors on weekdays and 50,000 on weekends.
