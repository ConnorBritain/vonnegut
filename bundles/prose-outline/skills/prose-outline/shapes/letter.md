  - occasion | required | 0.05-0.15
  - body | required | 0.60-0.85
  - ask | required | 0.05-0.20
good_for: newsletter, open-letter, note-to-team
length_hint: 200-1500
---
Written to someone, about something: the occasion that prompts it, what the
writer wants them to understand, and what the writer is asking of them. It
works when the relationship between writer and reader is part of the point and
the voice can be direct. It fails when the addressee is a pretext and the piece
is really an essay with a salutation on it.
