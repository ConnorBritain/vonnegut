A short framing, then the questions a reader would actually ask, each answered
in turn; the `question` part repeats, one node per question, and its share is
the sum of them. It works when the reader arrives with questions and wants to
skip to theirs. It fails when the questions are the writer's talking points
dressed up, or when the answers lean on each other so they cannot be read
alone.
