  - complication | required | 0.20-0.40
  - turn | required | 0.10-0.20
  - resolution | required | 0.10-0.25
good_for: essay, newsletter, story, talk
length_hint: 500-5000
---
A story told for what it shows: a situation, the moment it changes, what gets
harder, the turn where something is understood or decided, and where that
leaves the people in it. It works when one concrete story carries the idea and
the idea can arrive late. It fails when the setup runs long because the writer
is circling the point, or when the resolution explains what the story already
showed.
