---
name: problem-solution
mode: argument
parts:
  - problem | required | 0.15-0.30
  - stakes | required | 0.05-0.20
  - solution | required | 0.30-0.55
  - objection | optional | 0.05-0.20
  - close | required | 0.05-0.15
good_for: newsletter, essay, linkedin-post, memo
length_hint: 400-2500
---
Name a problem the reader recognises, say what it costs, propose what to do,
meet the obvious objection, and close on the first step. It works for proposals
and process changes, where the reader has to agree the problem is real before
any solution sounds necessary. It fails when the problem is stated in the
abstract, or the solution arrives before the stakes have made anyone care.
