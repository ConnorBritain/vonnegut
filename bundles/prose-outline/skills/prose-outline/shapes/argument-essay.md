The classic shape: say what you argue early, earn it with support, take the
strongest objection seriously, and end on what follows from it. It works when
the writer has one claim and several kinds of evidence for it. It fails as a
list of points that share a topic but not a claim, and when the counterargument
is a straw figure the reader can see through.
